## Full Description (4000 chars)
**The pregnancy app built for every woman, in every country.**

Bloom combines AI-powered support with essential pregnancy tools — contraction timer, kick counter, symptom tracker, mental health check-ins, and week-by-week baby development — all in one privacy-first app.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🌸 AI PREGNANCY COMPANION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ask Bloom anything about your pregnancy. Our AI knows your exact week, trimester, and health profile — so every answer is personal, not generic.

Worried about a symptom? Not sure if a food is safe? Wondering what your appointment will involve? Just ask.

Critical safety feature: Bloom detects emergency symptoms automatically and responds instantly — it will never delay your urgent question to wait for an AI model.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⏱️ CONTRACTION TIMER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Accurate hospital-quality contraction timing. One tap to start, one tap to stop. Bloom tracks:

✓ Duration of each contraction
✓ Intervals between contractions
✓ The 5-1-1 rule (every 5 min, lasting 1 min, for 1 hour)
✓ Automatic alert when it's time to go to hospital

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
👶 KICK COUNTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Count your baby's kicks with a single tap. The WHO recommends counting 10 movements — Bloom celebrates when you reach the goal and tracks your patterns over time. Low movement alerts help you know when to call your midwife.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📋 SYMPTOM & MOOD TRACKER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Log how you feel every day — nausea, fatigue, swelling, pain, mood, and 30+ more symptoms. Bloom surfaces patterns and highlights anything worth discussing at your next appointment.

🧠 MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1 in 5 pregnant women experience depression or anxiety. Bloom includes:

• Mood tracking and trend analysis
• Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) screening
• Crisis detection — immediate local emergency resources if you need them
• Localised support numbers for your country

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📖 WEEK-BY-WEEK DEVELOPMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

From poppy seed (week 1) to watermelon (week 40). Every week brings detailed updates on:

• Your baby's size, organs, and development milestones
• Your body's changes and what to expect
• What happens at your upcoming prenatal visits
• Nutrition and lifestyle tips for this specific week
