## Description (4000 chars)
Bloom is the pregnancy app built for every woman, in every country — not just the ones with premium healthcare.

Whether you're in week 6 or week 38, Bloom gives you week-by-week guidance, AI-powered support, and the tools midwives recommend — all in one beautifully simple app.

**🌸 AI PREGNANCY COMPANION**
Ask Bloom anything, any time. Our AI understands your specific week, trimester, and health history to give you personalised answers — not generic advice. Questions about nausea, movement, discharge, contractions, or what's safe to eat? Bloom has you covered.

Emergency? Bloom automatically detects urgent symptoms and responds with immediate guidance — it will never make you wait for an AI answer when your safety is at stake.

**📋 SYMPTOM TRACKER**
Log how you feel every day. Bloom learns your patterns and flags anything that deserves your midwife's attention. Track nausea, fatigue, swelling, pain levels, mood, and over 30 other symptoms with a single tap.

**🤰 CONTRACTION TIMER**
Hospital-quality contraction timing built right in. Track duration, frequency, and intervals. Bloom applies the 5-1-1 rule automatically and tells you when it's time to go in.

**👶 KICK COUNTER**
Count fetal movements with a single tap. Track your daily kick sessions and see your baby's movement patterns over time. Bloom alerts you if movement counts fall below what's expected for your week.

**🧠 MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT**
Pregnancy and the postpartum period can be emotionally challenging. Bloom includes evidence-based mood tracking, Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) check-ins, and crisis support resources localised for your country.

**📖 WEEK-BY-WEEK DEVELOPMENT**
Watch your baby grow from a poppy seed to a watermelon. Detailed week-by-week breakdowns of your baby's development, your body's changes, and what to expect at your next appointment.

**📝 BIRTH PLAN BUILDER**
Create a professional birth plan in minutes. Choose your preferences for pain relief, delivery environment, and newborn care. Export as a PDF to share with your care team.

**🏥 HOSPITAL BAG CHECKLIST**
Never forget anything. Bloom's smart hospital bag checklist knows your due date and reminds you when to start packing — with separate lists for you, your partner, and your baby.

**💑 PARTNER MODE**
Invite your partner to follow your pregnancy journey. They get their own view — baby size, weekly updates, how you're feeling today — so they can support you better.

**⚖️ WEIGHT & VITALS TRACKER**
Log your weight, blood pressure, and glucose readings. See your trends at a glance and share charts with your healthcare provider.

**🌍 BUILT FOR THE WHOLE WORLD**
Bloom works in 7 languages — English, Spanish, French, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, and Urdu. Healthcare guidance is tailored to your country's system, whether you're in the UK, US, Pakistan, Brazil, or anywhere in between.

**🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN**
Your pregnancy data is deeply personal. Bloom is built with privacy at its core:
• We never sell your data. Ever.
• End-to-end encryption for all health data
• Biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID)
• Full data export and account deletion at any time
• GDPR and HIPAA-aligned practices
• No data brokers. No advertising networks.

BabyCenter was named in a US Senate data-broker investigation. We built Bloom to be the opposite.

**📱 POSTPARTUM & BEYOND**
Bloom doesn't stop at birth. Track your recovery, your newborn's feeds and nappies, and complete your 6-week check-in with confidence.
